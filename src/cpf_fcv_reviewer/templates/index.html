How the Express review works

The reviewer reads the primary document, selects traceable evidence, checks the supporting and contextual material, and produces a connected note in prose.

  1. Extraction: reads the uploaded documents and identifies usable text.
  2. Evidence selection: gives the primary CPF the greatest analytical weight while retaining bounded corroboration from the other zones.
  3. Contextual checking: uses the package and wider context to make the feedback specific.
  4. Stage-sensitive synthesis: adjusts the scale of advice, from strategic options at Early drafting / PCN to fine-tuning at Finalization.
  5. Validation and rendering: checks evidence links and produces a Word-ready note.

Use public or non-sensitive material only. This tool provides advisory feedback and does not replace formal review or clearance processes.
